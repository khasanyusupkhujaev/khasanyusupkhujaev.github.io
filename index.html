About

I currently serve as the Advisor to the Director for Digitalisation and Artificial Intelligence at the Youth Affairs Agency of Uzbekistan, where I lead national initiatives focused on AI-driven youth development, digital services, and innovation ecosystems. Previously, I worked as a Data Scientist and Data Engineer at the Ministry of Digital Technologies of Uzbekistan (MITC) and lectured in Artificial Intelligence and Complex Systems at the International Digital University (IDU). I earned my M.Sc. in Data Science from Durham University as an El-Yurt Umidi Foundation (EYUF) Scholar, where I researched predictive modelling for cricket outcomes. I also hold a B.B.A. in Logistics from INHA University. My work focuses on building human-centered and socially impactful AI systems across Natural Language Processing, Machine Learning, and Reinforcement Learning, and I continue to pursue independent research toward a top PhD program in AI.
