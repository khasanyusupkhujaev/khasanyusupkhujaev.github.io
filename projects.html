Projects

Youth Research Office (YRO)
Data-driven youth policy research unit
Established and lead a compact research unit within the Youth Affairs Agency of Uzbekistan that transforms national youth data into clear, visual, and policy-ready insights. The initiative frames focused research questions on education, skills, employment, entrepreneurship, and social support, applying rigorous analysis and transparent data sources to produce accessible reports and visualizations that support evidence-based decision-making for youth development policies.
Uzbek LLM
First Uzbek-language large language model
Built the first Uzbek-language large language model from scratch, including data collection, preprocessing, training, and evaluation, addressing the challenges of low-resource NLP.
Smart Cradle (Beshique)
Raspberry Pi–based baby monitoring system
Developed a Raspberry Pi–based smart cradle that detects infant wakefulness using computer vision, audio analysis, and reinforcement learning. Implemented adaptive rocking mechanisms to soothe babies before they begin crying.
