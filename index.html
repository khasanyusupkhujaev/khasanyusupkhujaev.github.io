About

I am a Data Scientist and Data Engineer at the Ministry of Digital Technologies of the Republic of Uzbekistan, currently pursuing independent research with the goal of entering a top PhD program in AI. I earned my M.Sc. in Data Science from Durham University, where I worked with Professor Fred Worral. During my M.Sc., I conducted my dissertation on predictive modeling for cricket match outcomes. Before joining Durham, I completed my B.B.A. in Logistics at INHA University in Tashkent. My research focuses on advancing Natural Language Processing, Machine Learning, and Reinforcement Learning to build human-centered and socially impactful AI systems, with applications spanning low-resource language models, predictive analytics, and intelligent devices. In recognition of my academic excellence, I was awarded the El-Yurt Umidi Foundation Scholarship by the Government of Uzbekistan.
