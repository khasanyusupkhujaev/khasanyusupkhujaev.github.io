Projects

Youth Research Office (YRO)
Data-driven youth policy research unit
Established and lead a compact research unit within the Youth Affairs Agency of Uzbekistan that transforms national youth data into clear, visual, and policy-ready insights. The initiative frames focused research questions on education, skills, employment, entrepreneurship, and social support, applying rigorous analysis and transparent data sources to produce accessible reports and visualizations that support evidence-based decision-making for youth development policies. I serve as the project supervisor, leading the research direction and development of the initiative.
O’zbekiston Muhandislik Assotsiatsiyasi
First robotics and engineering association NGO in Uzbekistan
Lead and supervise an initiative focused on developing robotics and engineering among the youth of Uzbekistan through educational programs, research, practical training, competitions, and engineering projects. The association supports young engineers and robotics teams in developing technical skills and participating in international competitions. Among its achievements are 4th place at the FIRST Robotics Championship in Panama and 8th place at a NASA competition in Virginia, USA, alongside other international and national achievements.
Sherdor Games
First gaming accelerator in Uzbekistan
Lead and supervise a gaming accelerator program designed to support young game developers in Uzbekistan. The program enrolls 15 developers and provides stipends, meals, dedicated workspace, and high-performance computing equipment for game development, alongside mentorship and technical guidance. The program helps participants turn their ideas into complete games, with projects such as Uzb Parking, Walking Island, and Power of Desert already developed through the initiative.
Startup Fest
Startup and engineering festival for young innovators
Lead and supervise a startup and engineering festival that brings together young founders, engineers, and technology enthusiasts to transform ideas into real startups, engineering projects, robots, and emerging technologies. The initiative also includes the Catalyst program, which connects promising builders and project teams with major corporations and industries, including Yandex, IMZO, Hududgaz, and other leading organizations, creating opportunities for collaboration, mentorship, and real-world implementation.
Youth Platform
Unified digital platform for the youth of Uzbekistan
Lead and supervise the development of a unified digital platform for young people across Uzbekistan, bringing together information and services available to youth in education, employment, entrepreneurship, social support, and other areas. The platform provides nationwide vacancy listings, news and updates from the Youth Affairs Agency and its 100+ projects, personalized AI-powered tools for personal development, and access to various youth opportunities and incentives. The platform is available through both web and mobile applications for Apple and Android.
Chess Ticket for the 46th FIDE Chess Olympiad
Online and offline ticketing system for the FIDE Chess Olympiad
Led the development of the complete ticket purchasing system for the 46th FIDE Chess Olympiad held in Samarkand, Uzbekistan. The project included the design and implementation of both online and offline ticket sales infrastructure, enabling visitors to purchase and manage tickets through a unified system.
