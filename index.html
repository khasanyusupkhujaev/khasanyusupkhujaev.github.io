About

I am a Data Scientist and Data Engineer at the Ministry of Digital Technologies of Uzbekistan (MITC), and a Lecturer in AI and Complex Systems at the International Digital University. I earned my M.Sc. in Data Science from Durham University as an El-Yurt Umidi Foundation (EYUF) Scholar, where I researched predictive modeling for cricket outcomes, and hold a B.B.A. in Logistics from INHA University. My work focuses on Natural Language Processing, Machine Learning, and Reinforcement Learning for human-centered and socially impactful AI systems, and I am pursuing independent research toward a top PhD program in AI.
